# Google Play · English (translation)

## App name (max 30)
RecycleIQ: What goes where?
(Do not use "Athens" or "City" in the name: it reads as a government app.)

## Short description (max 80)
Snap a photo and learn which bin it goes in and where the nearest bin is.

## Full description (max 4,000)
Independent app: RecycleIQ is not an app of the City of Athens or any other public authority and does not represent them. Guidance is indicative. Bin reports and bulky-item requests are filed in the City of Athens public citizen request system (athensgov.cityofathens.gr).

Not sure where something goes? Take a photo. RecycleIQ tells you which bin it goes in, why, how to prepare it and where the nearest bin is in your neighbourhood.

SCAN AND LEARN
• Photograph an item or describe it in a few words: plastic bottle, pizza box, battery, old phone, sofa.
• Get the right bin: blue bin, glass bell, purple clothing bin, battery point or electrical-appliance point.
• If needed, it asks one question, such as "Is the box greasy?", so the answer is right.
• Learn what to do before you throw it away: empty it, rinse it, tape the battery terminals.

BULKY ITEMS AND LARGE APPLIANCES
• Sofa, mattress, wardrobe, fridge, washing machine: ask the city to collect them from your door from the app, or call 1595.
• Your address goes only to the city for the pickup. RecycleIQ does not store it.

FULL OR DAMAGED BIN?
• Tell the city in two taps. The report goes to the City of Athens citizen request system.

ASK RECYCLEIQ
• An assistant that answers in Greek, by voice too: "Where do batteries go?", "Where is the nearest recycling bin?".
• It uses open-source models and answers from the app's guidance and data.

BINS NEAR YOU
• Blue bins, glass bells, clothing bins, battery and electrical-appliance points in your neighbourhood, with walking time.

YOUR NEIGHBOURHOOD
• See what your neighbours scan and climb the neighbourhood leaderboard under a nickname.

YOUR PRIVACY
• No account, no name or email.
• Your location becomes a neighbourhood on your phone. Your coordinates never leave the device.
• Photos are recognised on the phone first. Only when the phone is not sure is a small image sent for recognition, and we do not keep it.
• No ads and no tracking tools.

RecycleIQ is an open-source pilot app for Athens by team StellarFirm. It started at the Apps4Athens 3.0 Hackathon.
